Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026.

  1. What we collect. Your name, email, company, trade, any scope quantities and qualifiers you include, and the plan files and plan-room links you send us. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  2. Why. Only to prepare and deliver your bid package and to communicate with you about it.
  3. Sharing. We share information only with the providers that run the service: Stripe (payments) and Formly.email (our form provider). We never sell your information or share it with other customers or competitors.
  4. Retention. Files are deleted 90 days after delivery unless you ask otherwise. Payment records are kept as required for tax and accounting.
  5. Your choices. Email us anytime to ask for a copy of your information or to have it deleted.
  6. Contact. Email bids@thumbbiddesk.com. Thumb Bid Desk is based in Michigan.
