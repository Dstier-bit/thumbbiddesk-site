Service Terms & Estimate Disclaimer

Effective October 22, 2026.

  1. Provider. Thumb Bid Desk is an assumed name of S&S Home Finishes LLC, a Michigan LLC. In these terms, "Provider" or "we" means Thumb Bid Desk. Contact: bids@thumbbiddesk.com.
  2. Services. Bid-package processing: organizing and reviewing bid documents the Customer provides (plans, specs, addenda, bid forms), and preparing a summary of scope, key dates, bid-form requirements, quantity takeoffs where the package type includes them, questions/RFIs to consider, and a checklist of submission requirements.
    • Small Package — $99. Up to ~25 sheets/pages of documents; painting, drywall or flooring only, one trade/scope.
    • Full Bid Package — $229. Larger or multi-trade packages.
    • Addendum Update — $39. Update a package we already delivered for one issued addendum.
    • Rush — +$59. Next-business-day delivery, added to a package price, only when capacity allows. Rush is a separate add-on checkout. If we can't fit a Rush order in, the $59 Rush fee is refunded in full.
  3. Turnaround. Standard delivery is within 2 business days after we receive a complete package and payment. Rush is by end of the next business day. Business days are Mon–Fri excluding US federal holidays, Eastern Time. A package is "complete" when we have every document listed in our intake confirmation. Packages over 300 pages get a custom turnaround that we confirm by email before work starts.
  4. Deadline floor. Packages must arrive at least 3 business days before the bid due date for standard service, or 2 business days for rush. We may decline packages received later; if we decline before work starts, you get a full refund. Meeting the owner's bid deadline is always Customer's responsibility.
  5. On-time guarantee. If we miss your bid due date, you get a full refund — except for delays or missed deadlines caused by internet, network, email, or service-provider outages. Thumb Bid Desk is not responsible for delays or missed deadlines caused by internet, network, email, or service-provider outages.
  6. Payment. Fees are paid in advance by card. Payments are processed through Stripe and may appear as THUMBBID on your card statement. Rush and addendum fees are separate charges. Package, Rush and addendum charges are one-time. Monthly plans (Bid Desk Monthly $399/mo: 2 Full or 4 Small packages plus addenda; Bid Desk Pro $749/mo: 4 Full packages, addenda and 1 Rush) renew automatically each month until cancelled by emailing bids@thumbbiddesk.com; unused credits don't roll over.
  7. Refunds, cancellation and corrections. One policy applies to every order: (1) Before work starts: if you cancel by emailing bids@thumbbiddesk.com, or if we cannot start your package, you get a full refund. (2) Rush: if we can't fit a Rush order in, the $59 Rush fee is refunded in full. (3) Our errors: if a delivered package has errors on our end, we fix them free; if we can't fix them, you get a refund within 30 days of delivery. (4) Otherwise there are no refunds after delivery. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
  8. Estimate disclaimer. Deliverables are estimating support, not a guaranteed price, engineering or architectural opinion, or legal advice. Quantities come only from the documents provided and may differ from field conditions. Customer is solely responsible for verifying quantities, pricing, labor rates, scope, bonding/insurance requirements, and compliance with the solicitation, and for the final bid amount and submission. Provider never submits bids, signs forms, or communicates with owners on Customer's behalf. The solicitation and its addenda control over our summary.
  9. AI disclosure. Work is prepared with AI-assisted tools and reviewed by a person before delivery.
  10. Limitation of liability. Provider's total liability for any claim is limited to the fees Customer paid for the specific package involved. Provider is not liable for lost bids, lost profits, or indirect or consequential damages.
  11. Confidentiality. Customer documents and pricing are confidential, used only to deliver the service, and never shared with other customers or competitors. Files are deleted 90 days after delivery unless Customer asks otherwise. Provider may use de-identified, non-pricing data to improve its process.

    • Separation. Provider may bid any project as a contractor. Customer documents, pricing and numbers are kept separate from Provider's own contracting estimates, are never seen by anyone who prepares those estimates, and are never used for Provider's own bids. This applies to every customer and every contractor whose bid is processed.
  13. Customer responsibilities. Customer confirms it has the right to share the documents, and is responsible for portal registration and compliance (e.g., SIGMA VSS, MITN/BidNet, plan rooms).
