Turnaround and what you'll receive

Standard turnaround is 2 business days from a complete package. Your package comes back ready to price: scope summary, takeoff, requirements checklist, clarifications list, and an organized submission folder, plus an estimate priced with industry standards. Send the package at least 3 business days before the bid is due for standard, or 2 for rush, so you have a day to price.

Rush (next business day): +$75, when capacity allows. If you added Rush, say so in your email.

Takeoffs and summaries are prepared with AI-assisted tools and checked by a person before delivery. You review, set your final price, sign, and submit your own bid.
